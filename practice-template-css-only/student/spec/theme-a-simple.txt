TEMPLATE A - SIMPLE
===================

Architecture
- Sticky horizontal navigation at the top.
- Main content max-width: 1120px; centered.
- Default nav link padding-inline: 18px.
- Compact nav (5+ direct nav links): padding-inline 10px; font-size 0.82rem.
- Sections use ordinary document flow; minimum spacing between panels: 72px.

Required variables (exact names and values)
--color-bg: #f6f7fb
--color-surface: #ffffff
--color-text: #172033
--color-muted: #667085
--color-accent: #3157ff
--color-border: #dfe3ec
--radius: 12px
--shadow: 0 14px 35px rgba(23, 32, 51, 0.10)

Typography and appearance
- Use a modern system sans-serif stack.
- Headings are bold and compact; body line-height is at least 1.6.
- Panels are white cards with subtle border and shadow.
- Buttons and active controls use --color-accent.

Signature effect - sweep-fill
- .button uses a pseudo-element that sweeps from left to right.
- The fill appears on both :hover and :focus-visible.
- Text remains readable throughout the transition.

Responsive
- Gallery: 1 column below 600px; 2 from 600px; 4 from 960px.
- With exactly 6 items, large items span two columns on desktop.
- At reduced motion, transitions and smooth scrolling are disabled.
